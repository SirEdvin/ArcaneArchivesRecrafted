# 0143 — Manifest tracking after source removal or replacement

## Original behavior

Pinned upstream `bb99accf48ed583e29b0efae56e28c963407b8df` stores selected item/location references in `ManifestTrackingUtils`; the inspected selection and line-rendering paths consume cached coordinates rather than continuously rediscovering their sources. Explicit selection/removal and dimension-clear paths change this cache.

The current port's `ManifestTracking.tick` prunes captured owner grants against current personal/Hive membership, as approved under 0140. `StorageNetworks.visible` independently rejects stale or replaced block entities, but tracking ticks do not consult current source grants. Consequently, unchanged membership alone cannot establish that a selected position still has an authorized storage source. This is source/control-flow evidence, not a newly executed replacement regression.

## Proposed behavior

Reconcile selected locations against current authorized sources when source changes can be confirmed:

- Remove a selection when its available, loaded storage location is removed or its last valid captured access grant disappears, including replacement by an inaccessible device.
- Preserve an independently valid personal or Hive Monitoring Crystal grant; duplicate monitors and opposite double-chest halves must not erase each other's still-valid access.
- Do not remove selections merely because chunks or dimensions are unavailable. Do not force-load chunks to validate tracking. Revalidate when sources become available again.
- Do not automatically restore a selection after confirmed revocation/removal; require a new selection, consistent with 0140.
- Do not rescan inventory contents to require the originally selected item to remain present. This remains cached location tracking, not a live stock reservation.
- A replacement that remains authorized is not automatically considered a privacy revocation solely because its Java block-entity instance changed. Avoid confusing legitimate deserialization with removal.

## Reason

Membership-based grants alone leave tracking attached to a location after its source or access path disappears. Bring source lifecycle handling into the already server-authoritative tracking model without turning chunk unloads into destructive clearing or removing independent access rights.

## Affected targets

Minecraft 1.20.1 Fabric/Forge and 1.21.1 Fabric/NeoForge. Tracking state and resulting S2C clear/revocation updates only; no storage mutation or new permission.

The confirmed loaded-location removal case is implemented; full source-grant/replacement reconciliation remains pending.
